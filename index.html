PROPERTY HOSTS WANTED

Could your property help extend the mesh?

A rooftop, tower, upper floor, or other elevated spot can help a community repeater reach more people nearby. A small amount of space in a useful location may fill a coverage gap. We can look at the area and talk through the details with you before you decide.

WHAT HOSTING CAN DO

  • Help connect neighbors and extend local radio coverage.
  • Support a community operated communications network.
  • Put an otherwise unused high point to practical use.

WHAT A TYPICAL SETUP LOOKS LIKE

  • Compact, low power radio equipment with an antenna.
  • Self powered solar or battery operation; no connection to building electricity is needed.
  • No internet or cellular service is needed for the radio repeater to relay local traffic.
  • We aim for simple, removable mounting with no roof holes or indoor cabling where the site allows.

Every property is different. Before installation, we can discuss the exact location, mounting method, equipment, access, and who will handle maintenance. There is no commitment in asking whether your site could help.
