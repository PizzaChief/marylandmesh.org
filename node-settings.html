MARYLAND MESH · NODE SETTINGS

Configure your node

A few settings determine which nodes your device can hear. Coordinate with your local mesh before changing radio settings.

1. Choose the correct region

The region setting selects the radio band your node is allowed to use. Choose the region that matches where you will operate; for Maryland, that is the US region (915MHz).

2. Match the local mesh

For Maryland Mesh’s common public mesh, use the US region, LongFast (LONG_FAST), and frequency slot 20 (906.875 MHz). The public primary channel is channel 0; frequency slot 20 is not “channel 20.” Private groups or events may use different channel details, so check before changing them.

Read the official local mesh guide →

3. Start with the usual device role

For most personal nodes, the CLIENT role is the normal starting point. Special roles such as ROUTER are for planned infrastructure nodes, not a general range boost.

Read the official role tips →

4. Keep private channel details private

Only share private channel links or QR codes with the people meant to join that channel. Check with your group before sharing channel details publicly.
